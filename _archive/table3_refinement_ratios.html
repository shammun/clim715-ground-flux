Range 8.2–10.2: a 1st-order error in Δt

CN's own 2nd-order accuracy is being masked by a separate error of order Δt1.

That separate error is the column ↔ SEB Lie operator splitting. Lie splitting is 1st-order regardless of what scheme runs inside each sub-step, so both BTCS and CN inherit a ratio near 10.

BTCS and CN errors at the same Δt differ by a factor of ~2 — that's CN's α = ½ weight cancelling half of BTCS's post-swing operator evaluation, the prefactor of the same 1st-order splitting term.

Test 1 result Damping-depth verification — all schemes match the analytical solution
Uniform soil column, sinusoidal surface forcing → FTCS, BTCS, and CN reproduce the analytical Ts(z, t) and G(0, t) to within sub-Kelvin RMSE.
Figure 2. Damping-depth verification. (a) Vertical profile of Ts at the time of peak surface forcing on day 5, top 100 cm only. (b) Surface temperature over the 24-hour cycle of day 5. (c) Surface ground heat flux G(0, t) for the same day. The three numerical schemes (FTCS at ν = 0.4, BTCS and CN at Δt = 900 s) and the analytical Fourier solution are visually indistinguishable, confirming the heat-conduction kernel is correctly discretized.

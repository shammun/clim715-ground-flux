Test 2 — small Δt At Δt = 15 s the three schemes are indistinguishable
Day-2 surface temperature on all three substrates — the three scheme lines overlap to plotting precision.
Figure 3. Day-2 surface temperature Ts(0, t) for the three substrates — asphalt road, concrete roof, bare soil — at Δt = 15 s, all three schemes plotted together. Air temperature Ta(t) is shown as a grey dotted reference. The three numerical schemes are visually indistinguishable on every substrate, confirming that at small Δt the time-stepping choice is irrelevant; substrate differences dominate (asphalt peaks > 50 °C while bare soil peaks < 35 °C).

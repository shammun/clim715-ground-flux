Top 5 cm at 12:00 LT: BTCS reads ≈ 2 K cooler than the FTCS reference; CN matches the reference within fractions of a kelvin.
Figure 5. Vertical temperature profile Ts(z) in the asphalt road column at three local times on day 2 (top 50 cm only). Reference is FTCS at Δt = 15 s. The schemes agree at 06:00 (steady cooling phase). At 12:00, where surface forcing peaks, BTCS underestimates the near-surface temperature by ≈ 2 K (the cool offset of its overshooting G is visible as a colder column), while CN tracks the reference profile closely. By 18:00 the schemes have re-converged. The error is concentrated in the top ≈ 5 cm, where the high κtop of asphalt produces a steep daytime gradient.

After κtop is partialled out, the strongest residual feature is bulk admittance μeff (mean |SHAP| = 0.082 K), with layer-interface descriptors smaller still.

The same parameter that sets the FTCS stability bound (κtop) also sets the prefactor of the BTCS coarse-Δt splitting error. Higher κtop → faster top-cell diffusion → larger Ts0 swing per step → bigger splitting error.
Reconciles the three-substrate ordering: soil κtop 2.3 × 10−7, asphalt 3.8 × 10−7, concrete 7.1 × 10−7 — error ordering follows κtop ordering.

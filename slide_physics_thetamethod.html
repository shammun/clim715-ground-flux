Setup 1-D heat equation on a stretched grid + the θ-method family

Governing equation & discretisation

Cs(z) ∂Ts/∂t = ∂/∂z [λs(z) ∂Ts/∂z]
Conductivity form preserves discrete heat content across material discontinuities, where λ and C jump by orders of magnitude.
Cell centres on a stretched grid (Δz1 = 0.5–1 cm at the surface, 30 cm at z = 2 m). Conductivity at half-levels via harmonic mean λj+½ = 2 λj λj+1 / (λj + λj+1) — preserves the flux across a λ jump.

The θ-method update

CjΔzj (Tjn+1 − Tjn)/Δt =
α [−∂G/∂z]n+1 + (1−α) [−∂G/∂z]n
One equation, three schemes: choose α.
α = 0FTCS
α = ½Crank–Nicolson
α = 1BTCS
Implicit cases (α > 0) need a tridiagonal solve each step — O(N) cost with the SciPy banded solver.
Top BC: SEB closure Rn − H − LE − G = 0, solved for Ts0 by Newton iteration to |ΔT| < 10−4 K. Bottom BC: Zero-flux Neumann at z = 2 m. Forcing: S↓ sinusoidal (1000 W m−2 peak), Ta mean 292.5 K + 7.5 K cosine, peak at 14:00. Order of ops: advance soil column with current Ts0 → update Ts0 from SEB with new T1.

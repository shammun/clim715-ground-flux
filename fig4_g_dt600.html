Test 2 — coarse Δt Day-2 G(t) at Δt = 600 s — FTCS blows up, BTCS overshoots, CN tracks the reference
Asphalt and concrete roof: FTCS at Δt = 600 s violates the stability bound and diverges. BTCS overshoots the daytime peak by 40 %. CN stays within a few W m−2.
Figure 4. Day-2 ground heat flux G(0, t) for the three substrates at Δt = 600 s, compared against the FTCS reference at Δt = 15 s (thick black). On asphalt and concrete, FTCS at Δt = 600 s blows up (off-scale, > 400 W m−2); BTCS overshoots the daytime peak by ≈ 40 % on asphalt and ≈ 25 % on concrete; CN remains visually tight on the reference. On bare soil all three schemes are nearly coincident because the much smaller κtop ≈ 3 × 10−7 m2 s−1 keeps ν well below 1 even at Δt = 600 s.

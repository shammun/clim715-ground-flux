Magnitude of the von Neumann amplification factor |Ak(ν, kΔz)| for the FTCS (left), BTCS (centre), and Crank–Nicolson (right) schemes applied to the heat conduction equation, plotted against the non-dimensional wavenumber kΔz for ν ∈ {{0.25, 0.5, 1, 5}}.
